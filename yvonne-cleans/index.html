Common questions

Frequently asked

Got a question we haven't covered? WhatsApp us — we usually reply in minutes.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp or call us on 072 238 9894. Tell us the size of the space, the type of clean, and when you need it. We'll send a clear quote — usually within 30 minutes.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Nope! Many of our clients give us a key or a code. We're fully insured, and the same trusted team will be at your place every time.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

100% yes. We use eco-friendly, non-toxic products that are safe for your whole family — including pets. No harsh chemical smells, just a fresh, clean home.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Pretoria and surrounding areas — including Centurion, Hatfield, Brooklyn, Menlyn, Garsfontein, Faerie Glen, and more. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

How long does a clean take?

Depends on the size and the type of clean. A standard 3-bed home takes 2-3 hours. A deep clean or move-out can take 4-6 hours. We'll always give you a time estimate upfront.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

We come back and re-clean for free. Your satisfaction is non-negotiable — that's the Yvonne promise.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes — we bring everything: vacuums, microfibre cloths, eco-friendly products, and our professional-grade extraction machine for upholstery. You don't need to lift a finger.
